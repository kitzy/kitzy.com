I haven't posted since September 3. That's ten weeks.

I noticed around week three. By week six I'd stopped telling myself the next post was coming soon.

## I bought a house

I bought a house. Then I moved into it, which turned out to be a separate and much longer project. Everything I own spent a while in the wrong place, and some of it still is.

I didn't expect it to take so much of my head. I can't hold a half-built argument while I'm trying to remember where I put the thing I need. The blog lost that fight every time.

## Audio

The other thing I love is audio engineering, and it didn't wait for me to finish unpacking. I've done two tours while still working my day job at Fleet.

Mixing a show takes everything I have while it's happening. I like that about it. It's the opposite of writing, where I get to go back and fix things. A live show only goes one direction.

I'm glad I get to do both. I also know that two jobs plus a move leaves nothing at the end of the day to point at a blank page.

## Fiction

I've been writing speculative fiction. It's a series of posts, and I'm publishing the first one tomorrow.

I'm not sure why fiction, or why now. Maybe the arguments I usually make felt too small for what I was reading. Maybe I needed to make something up for a while. Or maybe I just wanted to try something different.

## The world

I've also been overwhelmed. The state of the world, and everything around the midterms in particular.

I read all of it. I wrote almost none of it. I know the post I'd usually write, the one about systems and incentives and who benefits. I couldn't find the first sentence. Every time I got close, something else happened and the sentence felt out of date before I finished it.

I don't have a tidy way to say that, so I'm not going to give you one. I'm tired, and I'm still paying attention, and those two things have been pulling against each other.

## Back, for now

I'm not promising a schedule. I'm writing again, and I wanted you to know where I went first.

I haven't posted since September 3. Now I have.
